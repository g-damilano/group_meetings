Subject:	Safety Induction Recap: Key Information and Training Requirements

Dear all,
This email serves as a recap of the induction received over the last few weeks and to allow you to have a 
reference for the induction received over the last few weeks. It outlines the mandatory training, safety 
measures, and key information for all individuals working in laboratories or hazardous areas at Imperial 
College. If you have any doubts or need assistance, please reach out to a member of the group who will 
be able to help you.
1. Commitment to Safety
At Imperial College, safety is a top priority for all staff, students, contractors, and visitors. Senior 
management is fully committed to maintaining a strong safety culture, and your vigilance is essential to 
safeguarding personal property, equipment, and the building.
2. Mandatory Training Requirements
Before performing any lab activities, it is crucial that you complete the following online training:
*	Risk Assessment Foundation Training (RAFT): Required for post-docs and postgraduate 
students. Complete this within your first month, save the confirmation email, and store it in 
your safety folder. Complete the RAFT Training Here
*	Month One Safety Training (MOST): Required for staff and PhD students. Complete this within 
your first month and place the confirmation email in your safety folder. Complete the MOST 
Training Here
*	Fire Safety Awareness: It is compulsory to complete the Fire Safety Awareness training e-
learning as part of your induction. All access requests where the Hazardous Area Form indicates 
that this training is incomplete will be rejected. Inductors must not sign off forms with 
incomplete Fire Safety Awareness training. Complete the Fire Safety Awareness Training Here.
*	Fire Prevention and Fire Safety at Work: While not mandatory for access, it should be 
completed within a few weeks of starting. For more details and to register, please visit the 
training page here.
*	Computer Health Assessment: Ensures proper ergonomic practices for extensive computer 
work. If applicable to your work setting, this course must also be completed within your first 
month.
3. Emergency Arrangements
*	Emergency Contacts: In any emergency (e.g., fire, security incident, gas leak), contact Security 
Control at +44 (0)20 7589 1000 or 4444 from any phone (except Northwick Park: 3999; Silwood 
Park: 42444). Save these numbers in your phone. Alternatively, use the Safezone app.
*	Fire Safety: When the fire alarm sounds, evacuate the building immediately via the nearest exit 
and proceed to the designated assembly point. Do not return to the building until it is declared 
safe. Ensure that you are familiar with all emergency exits and assembly points. More details on 
fire safety can be found here. Additionally, please review the MSRH General Emergency 
Evacuation Plan (GEEP) here.
*	First Aid: First aid is available via Security or through local provisions in your work area. 
Familiarize yourself with the location of first aid kits and other emergency equipment. More 
details can be found here.
4. Good Laboratory Practices (GLP)
To minimize risks of contamination and injury, please observe the following:
*	Personal Hygiene: No eating, drinking, or applying cosmetics in the lab. Keep personal 
belongings, such as coats and bags, outside the lab.
*	Lab Attire: Lab coats, gloves, goggles, and other protective gear must be worn when necessary. 
For specific guidance on glove use, refer to this page. 
*	Housekeeping: Keep your workspace clean and dispose of waste properly. Report any spillages 
immediately and follow the correct clean-up protocol.
*	Risk Reduction: Plan all experiments carefully, identify potential hazards, and minimize the use 
of glassware and sharps where possible. Store heavy items at bench height.
6. Working Hours
*	MSci Students: Expected to work in the labs on Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays 
from 09:00 to 18:00 and on Wednesdays from 09:00 to 12:00 during the Autumn (7 Oct - 13 
Dec) and Spring (13 Jan - 21 March) terms.
*	BSc Students: Expected to work from 09:00 to 18:00 on Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays, and 
Fridays during lab weeks. See the attached ‘Key Dates’ document for more information on lab 
weeks.
*	Students are not permitted in MSRH laboratories outside of these hours.
*	Lone Working: Work involving hazardous materials must not be conducted alone. For more 
information on the Lone Working Policy, please refer to the guidelines here. If you need to work 
beyond 11 PM, apply for extended hours access through the Life Sciences webpage.
7. Shared Equipment and Hazardous Materials
When using shared equipment or working with hazardous substances, you must understand and follow 
proper safety procedures. This includes:
*	Glove Use: Gloves should not be worn outside hazardous areas. Replace gloves regularly and 
immediately if contaminated.
*	Emergency Procedures: Familiarize yourself with the location and correct use of spill kits, eye 
wash stations, and other safety equipment.
8. Reporting Accidents and Unsafe Practices
*	Accidents and Near Misses: Report any accidents, near misses, or unsafe conditions to your 
supervisor and on the College’s SALUS system to ensure corrective actions are taken. More 
information on reporting accidents can be found here.
*	Building Defects: Report any building defects or faults immediately. Further details on how to 
report these issues can be found here.
*	Reporting Unsafe Conditions: If you notice unsafe practices or conditions, immediately report 
them to your lab manager, Safety Adviser, or one of the following:
o	Security Control: 020 7589 1000
o	Estates Operations: 020 7594 8000
o	ICT Helpdesk: 020 7594 9000
o	Occupational Health: 020 7594 9401
o	Safety Department: 020 7594 9423
o	Safety Contacts: For a complete list of safety contacts, refer to this page.
9. Departmental and Local Rules
Familiarize yourself with the departmental safety personnel, local rules, and guidelines before starting 
work. Safety guidance and additional training needs will be provided by your local team or through the 
department's web pages.
