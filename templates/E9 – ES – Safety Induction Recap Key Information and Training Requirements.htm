1. Commitment to Safety

At Imperial College, safety is a top priority for all staff, students, contractors, and visitors. Senior management is fully committed to maintaining a strong safety culture, and your vigilance is essential to safeguarding personal property, equipment, and the building.

2. Mandatory Training Requirements

Before performing any lab activities, it is crucial that you complete the following online training:

  • Risk Assessment Foundation Training (RAFT): Required for post-docs and postgraduate students. Complete this within your first month, save the confirmation email, and store it in your safety folder. Complete the RAFT Training Here
  • Month One Safety Training (MOST): Required for staff and PhD students. Complete this within your first month and place the confirmation email in your safety folder. Complete the MOST Training Here
  • Fire Safety Awareness: It is compulsory to complete the Fire Safety Awareness training e-learning as part of your induction. All access requests where the Hazardous Area Form indicates that this training is incomplete will be rejected. Inductors must not sign off forms with incomplete Fire Safety Awareness training. Complete the Fire Safety Awareness Training Here.
  • Fire Prevention and Fire Safety at Work: While not mandatory for access, it should be completed within a few weeks of starting. For more details and to register, please visit the training page here.
  • Computer Health Assessment: Ensures proper ergonomic practices for extensive computer work. If applicable to your work setting, this course must also be completed within your first month.

3. Emergency Arrangements

  • Emergency Contacts: In any emergency (e.g., fire, security incident, gas leak), contact Security Control at +44 (0)20 7589 1000 or 4444 from any phone (except Northwick Park: 3999; Silwood Park: 42444). Save these numbers in your phone. Alternatively, use the Safezone app.
  • Fire Safety: When the fire alarm sounds, evacuate the building immediately via the nearest exit and proceed to the designated assembly point. Do not return to the building until it is declared safe. Ensure that you are familiar with all emergency exits and assembly points. More details on fire safety can be found here. Additionally, please review the MSRH General Emergency Evacuation Plan (GEEP) here.
  • First Aid: First aid is available via Security or through local provisions in your work area. Familiarize yourself with the location of first aid kits and other emergency equipment. More details can be found here.

4. Good Laboratory Practices (GLP)

To minimize risks of contamination and injury, please observe the following:

  • Personal Hygiene: No eating, drinking, or applying cosmetics in the lab. Keep personal belongings, such as coats and bags, outside the lab.
  • Lab Attire: Lab coats, gloves, goggles, and other protective gear must be worn when necessary. For specific guidance on glove use, refer to this page.
  • Housekeeping: Keep your workspace clean and dispose of waste properly. Report any spillages immediately and follow the correct clean-up protocol.
  • Risk Reduction: Plan all experiments carefully, identify potential hazards, and minimize the use of glassware and sharps where possible. Store heavy items at bench height.

7. Shared Equipment and Hazardous Materials

When using shared equipment or working with hazardous substances, you must understand and follow proper safety procedures. This includes:

  • Glove Use: Gloves should not be worn outside hazardous areas. Replace gloves regularly and immediately if contaminated.
  • Emergency Procedures: Familiarize yourself with the location and correct use of spill kits, eye wash stations, and other safety equipment.
